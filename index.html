Our Story

About VELLA CO.

Founded on the belief that true luxury is quiet, VELLA CO. creates clothing that speaks in silhouettes and textures rather than logos.

Every garment is designed in our atelier with obsessive attention to proportion, fabric and finish. We work only with the finest materials — Italian cashmere, Japanese silk, organic cotton — and partner with skilled artisans who share our respect for craft.

VELLA is for those who prefer presence over spectacle. Who understand that the most powerful statement is often the most understated.

Est. 2022 — Mumbai
